# Match Wars — The Runeborn Front

Play at `/game/matchwars.html`. The game is listed in the library's **Complete** shelf, alongside the polished games. Its route and existing `matchwars` catalog key are preserved.

## Latest expansion

- **50 new battles:** the campaign now reaches battle 134, with stronger champions and new boss spells beyond the original 84 battles.
- **Class mastery:** deployed campaign troops earn XP through six mastery ranks. Each of the 18 classes has two specializations, for 36 choices.
- **Rare rune events:** supply caches, frozen cells, and cursed runes occasionally change the next matching decision.
- **Shared daily challenges:** everyone receives the same seeded board, fixed army, mastery, and enemy waves. Earn medals and improve local personal bests; today's first victory grants 200 gold once.

## The battle loop

Select one of three lanes, then swap adjacent runes to make a line of three or more. The matched element summons your equipped class into the selected lane. Watch the incoming-enemy forecast and send counters where they will matter. Battles 1–12 have three waves, 13–32 have four, 33–56 have five, and 57–134 have six. A named boss guards the end of each chapter, including the final two-battle chapter at 133–134.

The campaign has **134 battles across 34 chapters**, adding 50 encounters beyond the previous 84-battle release. The first 84 names, descriptions, and established chapter metadata remain intact. Defeating the Eclipse Lord at battle 12, the Runeless Emperor at battle 48, or the Worldbreaker at battle 84 opens the next frontier. New campaigns start with 120 gold and an introductory primer, so you can choose an upgrade before the first wave. Earn more gold from enemy kills and battle rewards, buy upgrades in the forge, and revisit unlocked battles to try different army builds. Losing a battle lets you retry and adjust your strategy.

The campaign map shows one chapter at a time, with previous/next controls and a chapter selector. You can inspect locked frontiers, then continue at the highest unlocked battle. Later chapters add denser waves, elite enemies, mixed elemental fronts, and named boss variants. The last battle is against **The Eternal Unmaker**.

## Elements and troop roles

There are **18 playable classes**, three per element. Equip one class for each rune color in the class armory. The six original classes are available immediately; reaching campaign milestones unlocks twelve alternatives with distinct artwork, roles, and automatic spells. Class choices are free once unlocked. Changes made during a battle apply to the next battle, or to an immediate restart with the new army.

The main counter cycle is **Ember → Grove → Storm → Tide → Ember**. An arrow means the attacker is strong against the next element: a strong hit deals 1.5× damage, and attacking your weakness deals 0.7× damage. Sun and Void counter each other at 1.35× damage. Other matchups are neutral. The field guide and enemy forecast show these relationships during play.

A shielded Paladin in front of a Warden and Ranger holds a lane differently from a burst-heavy Mage and Lancer formation. Match the element you need, rather than automatically taking the first available match.

## Automatic spells and formation bonds

Every class casts its own spell when its cooldown is ready and suitable targets are in reach. Attackers burn, freeze, chain lightning, drain, or curse; supports heal, cleanse, shield, or hasten allies. Spell animations and status graphics distinguish these effects. Commander spells remain available through the on-screen buttons and Q / W / E.

Formation bonds activate while both listed elements have living allied troops **in the same lane**. They reward keeping complementary classes alive together; choosing two colors in the armory alone does not activate a bond.

| Bond | Elements | Benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Verdant Covenant | Grove + Sun | Healing and new shields in the lane are 20% stronger. |
| Shatter | Tide + Storm | Storm hits slowed, rooted, or frozen enemies for 25% more damage. |
| Soulfire | Ember + Void | Void hits burning enemies for 25% more damage. |
| Gravebloom | Grove + Void | Undead receive full healing; newly raised guards last four seconds longer. |
| Thunderous Aegis | Sun + Storm | Shielded allies attack 12% faster and move 10% faster. |
| Steamguard | Ember + Tide | Burning enemies deal 15% less attack damage; new allied shields are 10% stronger. |

Necromancers use fresh battlefield corpses rather than an unlimited summon supply. Each Necromancer can raise five guards; each enemy Grave Knight can raise three. Raised guards are temporary, leave no new corpse, and award no bounty, so they cannot produce a resurrection loop. Healing undead is weaker without the Gravebloom bond.

## Class mastery and specializations

Campaign battles award class XP for troops actually deployed and useful automatic spells. Both victories and defeats can grant participation XP; restarting an unfinished battle grants none. Daily runs use fixed training and do not award class XP. Every class has its own durable XP record, so changing your loadout does not erase previous training.

| Mastery rank | Total XP |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0 |
| 2 | 60 |
| 3 | 180 |
| 4 | 420 |
| 5 | 800 |
| 6 | 1,350 |

Each rank after the first adds a small health, attack, and automatic-spell cooldown bonus. At rank 3, choose one of two specializations in the class armory. For example, an Ember Mage can extend its burns or spread its blasts, and a Necromancer can strengthen raised guards or raise more of them for longer. The armory describes all 36 branches. Specializations are free to change and apply when the next battle begins; the current army keeps its starting mastery snapshot.

## Combos, specials, and relics

Cleared runes fall under gravity and refill from above. New matches cascade automatically; each additional chain strengthens the troops it summons. Larger matches summon more troops and can create elite units. Repeatedly fielding one element builds its resonance, adding another reason to specialize when the enemy composition allows it.

| Match or item | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Four in a horizontal line | Creates a row-blast rune. |
| Four in a vertical line | Creates a column-blast rune. |
| Five or more in a straight line | Creates a wild rune. Swap it with a rune to clear that element. |
| Intersecting T or L matches | Creates a nova rune with an area blast. |
| Two wild runes swapped together | Clears the board. |
| Fury relic | Adds attack power to troops summoned by that element's match. |
| Aegis relic | Adds shields to the summoned troops. |
| Haste relic | Improves movement and attack speed. |
| Vitality relic | Increases summoned troop health. |

Specials activate when swapped and can trigger other specials inside their blast. The rune's shape, element color, and relic badge communicate different properties. A move that makes no match is reversed. If no moves remain, the board safely reshuffles.

From battle 5 onward, rare events can appear on newly falling runes. The initial board is clear of events; at most one can be active, and new events are separated by at least eight successful moves. Invalid swaps do not advance event timers.

| Rare event | Decision and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Supply cache | Match or blast the rune to collect 20 battle gold and Overdrive charge. |
| Frozen cell | Cannot be directly swapped. Match the frozen rune, clear an adjacent rune, or use a special blast to thaw it. |
| Cursed rune | Clear it within four successful moves for empowered summons. Its birth move is free; expiry calls one enemy reinforcement. |

Rare events remain tied to their runes through gravity and reshuffles. Daily events contribute charge and scoring opportunities; their battle gold does not create an extra persistent daily payout.

## Enemy variety and upgrades

There are **33 base enemy types**: five regular roles for each of the six elements and three boss archetypes. Later chapters build on them with elite troops and 34 distinct named boss encounters. Threats include flying Harpies, regenerating Colossi, armored siege troops, shield bearers, healers, shield breakers, ranged casters, and summoners. Their traits matter as much as their element.

Twelve additional enemies have their own animated sprite graphics and automatic abilities: Ember Hound and Ash Witch; Tide Crab and Siren; Briar Spider and Spore Alchemist; Storm Beetle and Zephyr Djinn; Solar Chimera and Inquisitor; Grave Knight and Soul Leech. They introduce pounces, curses, shell shields, control songs, webs, poison clouds, static barriers, cyclones, holy attacks, resurrection, and siphoning.

Beyond battle 84, champions gain visible affixes with actual defensive or offensive effects: Warded, Frenzied, Mender, Hexer, Siphoner, or Ironhide. New bosses cast Sovereign Roots, Royal Whiteout, or Ruin Pulse according to their archetype. Later bosses can trigger two bounded shield-and-rally phases, increasing pressure without resetting their health. Elemental counters, control, shield breaking, and support become more valuable as formations grow stronger.

The forge offers **18 upgrade paths**, each with three ranks: two talents per element and six command upgrades. Element talents support the equipped classes through effects such as burn, splash, slow, healing, poison, lightning targets, shields, armor, life stealing, and execution damage. Command paths improve combo power, relic strength, gold rewards, keep health, spellcraft, and keep sentries. Rising rank costs make spending a strategic choice.

Meteor damages enemies in the selected lane; Rally empowers its allies; Renew restores the lane's allied troops and keep health. Spell cooldowns provide tactical support while you build the next match.

## Shared daily challenges

The Daily Challenge briefing presents a UTC date, the fixed six-class army, and bronze/silver/gold score targets before play. Six curated armies rotate through battles 12, 24, and 36. Every daily run has rank-2 forge talents and rank-3 class mastery with predetermined specializations, regardless of a player's campaign progress. The rune board and combat use separate deterministic random streams, so consuming board randomness cannot change the enemy stream.

Scores reward victory, defeated enemies, matches, cascades, rare-event resolutions, citadel health, and faster clears. A victory earns at least bronze; a defeat can retain a partial personal score but cannot earn a medal. Personal bests, medals, attempts, and claimed rewards are stored on this device for the most recent 30 UTC dates. These are shared-seed challenges with local records, not an online leaderboard.

Today's first victory grants **200 gold once**. Replays can improve a personal best but grant no further gold. Past dates are practice and grant no gold. Daily runs do not clear campaign battles or grant class mastery, and daily battle gold is not paid out separately. A run finished after its UTC day ends becomes practice for reward purposes.

Use the briefing's copy-link control to share `/game/matchwars.html?daily=YYYY-MM-DD`. Share links accept today and the previous 29 UTC dates, reject future or malformed dates, and open the selected briefing. Replaying the same date restores the same starting configuration.

## Mobile and PC controls

- **Touch:** swipe between adjacent runes, or tap one rune and then its neighbor. Tap the lane buttons before matching to direct summons. Spell buttons, the forge, campaign, and field guide are available on screen.
- **Mouse:** drag between adjacent runes, or click one rune and then its neighbor.
- **Keyboard:** focus the board and use arrow keys to navigate; Enter selects a rune. Number keys 1–3 select lanes. Q, W, and E use Meteor, Rally, and Renew. H shows a possible match. P or Escape opens the pause menu.

The layout adapts from a side-by-side desktop war room to a stacked mobile layout. The game menu includes sound and reduced-motion settings. Opening a dialog or leaving the game tab pauses combat so you can inspect troops and make upgrade decisions.

## Saving and compatibility

Progress saves locally on the current device and browser after purchases, battle results, stage selection, class selection, specialization selection, and settings changes. Campaign progress, gold, purchased upgrades, equipped classes, class XP, specializations, recent daily records, and settings survive reloads. Refreshing restarts the selected campaign battle with a new rune board; daily replays use their date's seed. Active troops, spell cooldowns, and unfinished battle rewards are not saved. The earlier Color Clash save is migrated into the new progression when available, preserving gold, a bounded campaign level, and mapped troop-upgrade ranks. The original save remains intact. Browser storage can be unavailable in restrictive/private browsing sessions; the game remains playable and reports a saving limitation.

Existing Match Wars saves retain their gold, upgrades, settings, equipped classes, and cleared battles. A save that completed battle 12 unlocks battle 13; a completed battle 48 unlocks battle 49; a completed battle 84 unlocks battle 85. New mastery and daily-record fields receive safe defaults without regranting battle rewards. The campaign ends at 134. The existing `matchwars-runeborn-v2` storage key remains in use.

The implementation uses native ES modules and Canvas 2D, with no game-framework download. Serve the repository over HTTP when testing locally; opening the HTML directly as a `file:` URL can prevent browsers from loading modules.

## FAQ

**Why did my swap move back?** It did not make a match and did not activate a special. You can use Hint to find an available move.

**Why are my troops struggling against a particular enemy?** Check both its element and its role. Use elemental counters, Storm against flyers, melee pressure against ranged-resistant troops, and support behind a shielded frontline.

**Can I change the lane of troops already deployed?** Lane selection directs subsequent summons and spells. Plan the lane before taking a match.

**Where is my progress on another device?** Saves live in this browser's local storage. There is no account-based cloud synchronization.
