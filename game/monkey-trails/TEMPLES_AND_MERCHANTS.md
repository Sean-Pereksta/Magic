# Directional temples, pack merchants and equipment smelting

Special destinations remain saved on each region's fantasy map. The first destination keeps the original regional landmark. The next four introduce east, west, south and north temples; later discoveries can include any of them.

| Temple | Direction | Initial goal | Guaranteed reward, plus a mystery reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dawnstride | East | 500 ft | 2 Light essence + 1 Bond Charm |
| Duskpath | West | 750 ft | 2 Shadow essence + 1 Treasure Scent |
| Sunfire | South | 1,000 ft | 2 Fire essence + 1 Spark Tonic |
| Northwind | North | 1,250 ft | 2 Ice essence + 1 Revival Seed |

Each later region adds 100 feet to these goals. Visit the temple to begin. Only accepted movement in the named direction counts; diagonal travel contributes its directional component. Other directions do not erase progress. Challenges pause in battles, at camp, or in another region, and survive reloads and expedition restarts. The longer any-safe-route option doubles the goal, keeps earned feet, and has identical rewards. The mystery reward table is shown before claiming: 45% essence, 35% materials, 15% rare gear, 4% epic gear and 1% legendary gear. Each spawned temple pays once.

## Merchant purchases

Visit a Wandering Merchant or the Mirage Bazaar, then choose **Browse merchant wares**. Choose pack material quantities for payment. Materials are reserved until Buy; previews do not spend them. Fruit, essence, charm materials, ingots, ore, fragments, bond materials and uncommon materials use their exact forge values. Existing equipment is excluded from merchant tender.

Stock includes berries, revival seeds, Bond Charms, regional essence, ingots and Star Ore. Prices are the offered item's crafting value with a 25% markup, rounded up; buying and reselling cannot generate credit. Any excess payment remains as saved merchant credit. Stock is limited per spawned merchant. Purchases, pack debits, stock and receipts commit together; failed device saves offer a retry without another purchase.

## Smelting equipment

The forge Equipment filter includes individual equipment copies. Drag or tap a spare copy to add it to the furnace, combine it with materials if desired, review the odds, and press Smelt. Base power by rarity is 8 / 12 / 18 / 26 / 36 / 48, plus 2 per rank above one. Elemental affinity also influences the outcome.

Equipped, favorited and protected copies stay inspection-only. Each selected copy is consumed only on a successful smelt. Receipt retries and encrypted reloads retain the original result after the ingredient copy is gone. A result matching the consumed item creates or upgrades only equipment that still exists after payment.

## Fruit baskets

Occasional accepted discoveries in grove habitats, and meadow paths within Emerald Jungle or Moonlit Forest, reveal a fruit basket at the discovery's fantasy-map position. Cave, wetland, ruins and ridge discoveries do not spawn baskets. Each region has one active basket; new spawn progress pauses while it is waiting.

A basket stores a reward of 3–5 fruit. Walk another 500 accepted feet in that region in any direction, then press Collect. Walking before the spawn never counts. Progress pauses during battles and at camp, survives reloads/restarts, and remains with its region. The map marker and Walk card show the basket and feet remaining. Repeated collection or save retry cannot pay twice.
