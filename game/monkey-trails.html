Field guide · location, compass, saves & safety

Explore your land

Walking north, south, east or west moves you the same way through the fantasy terrain. Map north stays up. Regions opens the illustrated region picker. The terrain is fictional; choose your own physical route. Regions and habitats shape wild encounters; the compass adds a gentle preference among eligible companions. North favors Leaf, south Fire, east Storm, and west Water. Earth, Ice, Light and Shadow also arrive through discoveries and resource exchange, so one safe route can complete the collection. Radar rings show relative encounters, not roads or accessible land. Select “Cannot safely reach this location” to hunt the same Ultra along a route you choose.

Foreground location only

Use the HTTPS-hosted game, allow location, and tap Start walking. Only accepted readings at 10 mph or below count. Weak GPS, jumps, stale readings, long gaps, and stationary drift are discarded. Background tabs, a locked screen, battles pause tracking. The Team, Tribe and other menus keep walking active. Resume manually; untracked gaps never earn distance. GPS is approximate, not cheat-proof.

Saved exploration

Cloud checkpoints and backups include your relative land position and a short exploration trail, with no latitude or longitude. A short-lived Ultra map anchor is separately encrypted on this device; reopening elsewhere recalibrates without changing the Ultra. Local and cloud checkpoints contain encrypted game progress. Existing shared Firestore rules do not make these saves owner-only: encryption protects readable content, but cannot stop another authenticated client from altering or deleting a record. Use a unique password and keep backups. Scores are casual and unverified.

Look up, then level up

Walk only in safe, permitted places. Never use the game while driving or crossing roads. Stop somewhere safe for battles and menus. The compass is a resource guide, not a navigation instruction. You never need to enter private or unsafe areas. Optional screen wake-lock may keep the display awake; otherwise keep it unlocked yourself.
