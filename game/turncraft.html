Turncraft Controls

Orders persist. Scout through true fog of war, pursue assigned targets, and win the objective selected by the pre-match vote.

Camera and selectionDrag to pan, use arrows, or W/S/D and [ (left), and wheel or pinch to zoom. F or Focus Selected returns to your selection. Hold still for a moment, then drag a box to select troops with mouse or touch. Shift-drag selects immediately; double-click or double-tap selects visible units of one type.
Touch ordersTap anywhere in your troop’s square to select it. Then tap ground to move, an enemy to attack, or another friendly troop to switch. Hold still on a friendly troop to add or remove it; hold then drag for a selection box. Double-tap selects visible troops of that type. Buttons for Mine, Build, and abilities take priority. Camera drags and pinches never issue orders. A or X arms Attack.
Persistent movementMove always takes priority over autonomous combat. Groups gather in the closest available squares around the destination and wait briefly for traffic. Hold and Stop never chase enemies.
Attack mode pursuitAttack an enemy to enable attack mode and chase it outside weapon range. When that target falls, the unit acquires the nearest visible enemy within its weapon range or 7 tiles. Move, Stop, or Hold turns attack mode off.
Troop colorsEnemies using your faction have a subtle red tint so you can tell them apart. Your troops, teammates, and enemies using other factions keep their original artwork.
Fog and scoutingEvery square outside friendly vision is fully dark. Fast scouts and advanced air units reveal farther than workers and basic troops.
Match objectivesAnnihilation destroys bases, Conquest holds three impact zones, and Central Relic requires carrying the relic home three times.
Frontier bunkerFrontier infantry can enter a nearby Bunker. Each garrisoned troop strengthens its weapon; select the Bunker to unload everyone.
Brood creepBrood structures spread purple creep. Brood units regenerate much faster while standing on it, and most Brood structures require it.
Group ordersAfter multi-selecting, choose Move Group then a destination. A/X or Attack Group followed by ground makes each troop seek its nearest visible enemy within twice its aggro range; otherwise it advances and engages enemies in aggro range. Clicking an enemy focuses that target. Hold a numbered group button to assign your selection; tap it to recall.
Expansion economyMinerals and gas require a completed friendly base within 5 tiles. Tap resources to spread selected workers; if a worker occupies the square, choose Mine first. Select or place a base to see its collection circle. Choose any structure and click a highlighted open site, even far from your worker. The worker walks over and starts construction if the site remains available and you can still afford it. Command Hubs, Nexuses, and Hatcheries each cost 300 minerals. Destroying a base disconnects its deposits.
Ability keysQ / E / R / T and the keys shown on each ability activate the selected units. W still pans the camera. Right-click or Escape cancels targeting. Hold an icon on touch for details. Unit abilities share their cooldown.
Projectile impactRanged damage now resolves when the shot visually reaches its target, not when the firing animation begins.
