Your first council

  1. Grow your realm. Select a gold-bordered tile and build. Farms feed people and armies; forests and hills provide materials. Each turn gives 3 construction or recruitment orders. Workshops add more.
  2. Command your banners. Select an army, press March, then select its destination. Roads speed travel. Declare war before entering a rival's land, or negotiate an alliance for access.
  3. Win the council. Open Houses, speak to a ruler, and use the treaty desk. Actions build trust; repeated praise and gifts lose influence. Review counteroffers, then ratify. Gemini is optional; every treaty is available without it.
  4. Keep your word. Use Give My Word to confirm a proposed oath. Open Pledges to deliver promised resources. Build an Envoy Office for 4 dispatches and an ambassador; a Royal Chancery allows 5 and three ambassadors. A stationed ambassador gives 10 separate messages with their host. Allies march, defend, withdraw and build under the same rules as you.
  5. Resolve the round. Give your construction, recruitment and army orders, then press End Turn. Every surviving rival takes one turn: it assesses supplies and threats, spends its own orders, and directs its armies. All armies then move and fight; construction, production and upkeep resolve for every House. New orders and dispatches become available for the next round. Realm → Rival Turns shows public activity. Private council priorities and unobserved orders require intelligence.
  6. Plan a sustainable campaign. Secure food and gold before enlarging your army. Build the required barracks, ranges, stables and siege works. Match formations to your troops, bring siege equipment against walls, and use roads to reinforce a threatened frontier. Rivals follow these same rules and can fight each other as well as you.
  7. Gather intelligence. Build a Whisper Office under Government. Open Intel to recruit named spies, send them to foreign courts, and choose missions. Reports contain real but incomplete information observed on their stated turn; plans can change. Upgrade to Level II for counterintelligence and Level III for five spies.
  8. Attack infrastructure. Select your army, then an enemy building. Structure Targets offers Attack and, for ranged siege engines, Bombard. Same-tile attacks remain attacks. Defenders must be defeated before the structure takes damage. Destroyed industry stops producing.
  9. Take the crown. Hold 60% of settlements, or keep alliances with a majority of surviving rivals for 3 turns. Losing your final settlement ends the campaign.

City walls require a siege. Infantry can wear walls down slowly at a high cost. Mixed armies travel at the speed of their slowest unit. Drag or pinch the map; use +/− to zoom. Arrow keys select tiles, Enter inspects, Home centers your capital.
