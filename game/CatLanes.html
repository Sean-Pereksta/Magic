A little field guide

Win the rift

Escort your waves through a lane. Break its outer tower, then its inner tower, to expose the enemy crystal. Destroy the crystal to win.

Make a power play

Jungle creatures give XP. The great beasts awaken in the river pits between lanes. Your team's finishing blow summons that beast to push a lane for you.

Grow your hero

Level up to 18 through your own kills and assists. Nearby teammates do not share your XP. Skills unlock at levels 1, 2, 4, and 6; talents at 3, 5, 8, 10, 12, 15, and 18. Retreat to your healing well, or recall to recover health and mana.

Build your loadout

Only your finishing hits earn gold, including minions and jungle creatures. Open SHOP or press I to buy permanent match upgrades. Start with 10 basic items, unlock 10 upgrades from them, then combine a starter and an upgrade to unlock each of 10 relics. All purchased items and their requirements stay active, even after death. The shop pauses your match.

Touch

Move with the left stick. Drag a skill button to aim, then release to cast. Or tap a skill, then tap the battlefield. Tap ATTACK or the battlefield to fight the nearest nearby enemy, or tap an enemy to focus it. Attacking continues after release and switches to the next nearby enemy when your target falls. If none is nearby, your hero waits in place. Ground taps attack; use the stick to move. Moving or issuing another order cancels it. Tap SHOP beneath your status panel to spend your gold. Hold or drag the mini map to look around; release to return to your hero. Tap CALL THE PLAY for team orders. Adjust control size, height, inset, or handedness from Pause → Touch controls.
