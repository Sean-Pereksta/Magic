A little field guide

Win the rift

Escort your waves through a lane. Break its outer tower, then its inner tower, to expose the enemy crystal. Destroy the crystal to win.

Make a power play

Jungle creatures give XP. The great beasts awaken in the river pits between lanes. Your team's finishing blow summons that beast to push a lane for you.

Grow your hero

Skills unlock at levels 1, 2, 4, and 6. Pick a talent at levels 3, 5, 8, and 10. Retreat to your healing well, or recall to recover health and mana.

Touch

Move with the left stick. Drag a skill button to aim, then release to cast. Or tap a skill, then tap the battlefield. Tap ATTACK to pursue the nearest enemy, or tap an enemy to focus it. Attacking continues after release and switches to the next enemy when your target falls. Moving or issuing another order cancels it. Tap the mini map to call a push, defense, or beast hunt.

Call the play

Allies respond when healthy. They still protect the crystal and retreat from losing fights.
