Light means danger

Flashlights and tower beams reveal any ape they touch. Keep to shadows and trees. Humans shout, run to alarms, and call reinforcements by radio. Interrupt a caller before help arrives. Break sight and move away from the last place you were seen.

Free your kin

Captive apes wait in cages around human camps. Strike a cage until it breaks, then call with Q to gather the freed apes. A large horde can overwhelm a patrol, but a crowded road is easy to spot. Hold your tribe in safe cover and scout alone when the hunt is hot.

Give your tribe a home

Settle on safe open land near berry bushes. Builders spend timber to expand the lodge and build huts around it. Each hut shelters apes and has its own health: human raiders and explosives can damage or destroy it. Defend your homes; after an attack, builders spend timber to repair and rebuild. Families need food, safety, and spare housing; gardens help communities thrive. Young apes grow up in 35 seconds. Your tribe can reach 1,000 living apes, including residents and young. Open the map to choose work priorities and deliver food. Choose Find Settlement or press V to show an edge arrow for every settlement, including homes whose residents have joined your horde, with distance and attack warnings.

Turn the hunt against them

Healthy adult apes can take a few ordinary hits, but concentrated gunfire still tears through a tribe. Use cover, then strike together. Military facilities hold large groups of captives; liberating them also wins food supplies. Your king slowly recovers when safe. Later marksmen can kill an ape in one shot: watch their aiming line and move away from its endpoint. Orange circles warn of grenades. Flank shield guards or swarm them. Medics support nearby soldiers. At 120 followers, mechanized squads hunt your army. At 200, military mobilization unlocks tanks. Leave the cannon’s warning circle before the shell arrives. Tanks resist frontal attacks: flank their rear or swarm the hull to disable the turret. Ambush APCs and troop trucks before their soldiers dismount. Larger bases have layered barricades; strike radios, fuel, barracks and depots to weaken the regional response. Forests remain your shelter.

Commission your village

Stand beside the main hut and choose Build or press B. The menu pauses the world while you choose. Pay village timber and food to commission spear towers, training facilities, defenses, housing, gardens, stores, and workshops. Builders clear the plot and finish the structure; work pauses during an attack. Larger villages unlock more building slots. Staffed training facilities gradually improve residents' health and strikes, and their training stays with them when recruited.

Let your tribe use its strengths

Bold apes favor front-line troops and armor. Guardians defend the king and their village. Saboteurs break radios, alarms, depots, and defenses. Rescuers seek captive cages; skirmishers target snipers, officers, and medics. Spread charges give them room to flank. Adult capuchins, gibbons, and chimpanzees climb lower walls; training helps other adults cross basic walls. Young apes stay on the ground, and reinforced fortress walls require a breach or gate. Keep striking when beside a wall to break it and open a route for the rest of the tribe.

Playing by touch

Use the left stick to move and the right button to strike. Open Commands to rally, hold, recall, settle, patrol, or attack nearest foes. Choose Charge or Spread charge, then tap the ground to send the horde in that direction. Tap Build beside the main hut to commission village structures. Tap Find Settlement to follow the edge arrows home; tap again to hide them.
