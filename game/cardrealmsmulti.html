How to play & read cards

Play cards to gain trade and combat. Spend trade to buy cards; spend combat to attack bases or your opponent's health (authority). Outposts protect their owner's health until destroyed.

Ally bonuses trigger with 2+ cards of the same faction. Scrap permanently removes that card for its bonus. Base effects repeat each turn.

Hover or focus a card for its rules. Tap its i button to inspect safely. Press I to keep details open, Esc to close, and Enter or Space to use a focused card.

Turn timer: 60s. If a player doesn’t end their turn, it auto-ends.
