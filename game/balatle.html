Hand guide & match rules

Match rules
  1. There are 5 rounds. Rounds 4 and 5 are Boss rounds.
  2. Each round has a 2:00 play timer. If time expires and you’re below target, you’re eliminated.
  3. Once you reach target, you can keep scoring for a bigger total, or click Finish Round to enter Buy.
  4. Buy Phase lasts 1:00 (or less if the match advances early). Buys only affect your run.
  5. Next round starts when all surviving players are Ready (or timeouts force it).
  6. End of Round 5: highest Total among survivors wins. If only one player remains, they win immediately.
Make every card count
A Pair always starts at 10 chips × 2 Mult, whether you play two cards or five. Only the cards making the poker hand add their chip values. Extra cards still activate jokers that explicitly reward cards played. Jokers trigger from left to right: move a ×Mult joker after your +Mult jokers to grow your score.
Use 1–9 to select cards, Enter to play, D to discard, and Esc to clear or close.
