    IN APP means the station exposes a public direct audio stream the browser can try without a login or navigation. It does not mean every football game is permitted on that internet stream.

    How playback works

    Catnmice now treats “Play” literally: audio must play inside this app. It searches mapped flagship and alternate station names in a public radio directory, keeps healthy HTTPS streams, and tries them one by one after any verified game-specific feed.

    If every in-app candidate fails, a separate glowing External options button appears. Provider pages such as iHeartRadio, Audacy, SiriusXM or NFL+ are never opened by the Play button and remain hidden until that point.

    No provider credentials, subscriber sessions, protected stream URLs, geographic restrictions or blackouts are bypassed.
