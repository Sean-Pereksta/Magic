COMMANDER'S HANDBOOK

A little weird. A lot of strategy.

Battles begin automatically after a 3–2–1 countdown. Every creature has its own action cooldown and one to three independent special abilities.

Before battle, spend your starting coins on 1–8 creatures including a commander. Select each purchased creature and place it in your two home rows. Custom games offer 12–40 starting coins; campaigns use 24.

  1. Choose a creature. Its portrait, health bar, and every special icon appear in the compact command bar. Single-player controls sit below the board.
  2. Use the grid. Blue means movement, red means damage, green means healing, and gold means support. Choose a different skill icon to see its targets. Only healing skills show green.
  3. Watch the recharge. Movement, attacks, and guard share a 2–3.5 second cooldown. Each special recharges separately in 8–12 seconds. Hover an icon or open creature details for its exact effect.
  4. Win your mode. Flag Capture keeps ownership after you leave. Zone Control counts troops within one orthogonal tile of each flag; tied zones are contested and do not score. Every 6 seconds held flags give light and supplies. Last Army Standing compares surviving health when time expires.
  5. Use your faction. Brood troops move farther from groves and recover there. Carnival scouts phase through enemy formations. Most Choir troops fly over obstacles and can land on water; nearby Choir allies build shields.
  6. Play together. Choose “2 players · same screen” for simultaneous local play. Player 2 has the top bar; Player 1 has the bottom. Both can drag their own creatures with separate fingers. This mode has no AI.

Menu contains pause/resume, creature details, sound, the journal, and new battle. Only Reinforcement Clash permits extra purchases during battle. Other modes use your starting warband and creatures summoned by special abilities. Story and Relaxed give the AI a longer grace period and much slower decisions.

Player 1: 1 Move · 2 Attack · 3/4/5 Specials · G Guard.
Player 2: Q Move · W Attack · E/R/F Specials · T Guard.
Space pauses both armies · Esc cancels selection. Tab and Enter also select tiles.
