COMMANDER'S HANDBOOK

A little weird. A lot of strategy.

Battles begin automatically after a 3–2–1 countdown. Each army starts with one order. Gain one every 2.4 seconds and bank up to two. Moving, attacking, bracing, casting or recruiting spends one. Save your second order for an ally’s follow-up or an enemy threat.

Choose your army, then tap Arrange troops to see the actual battlefield. Spend your starting coins on 1–8 creatures including a commander. Each faction offers seven distinct troops, including guardians, rangers and terrain-shaping specialists. Select each purchased creature and place it in your two home rows. Tap a roster creature then a home square, drag a creature, or use Auto arrange. Start battle when your formation is ready. Custom games offer 12–40 starting coins; campaigns use 24.

  1. Choose a creature. Its portrait, health bar, and every special icon appear in the compact command bar. Single-player controls sit below the board or beside it in short landscape windows.
  2. Use the grid. Blue means movement, red means damage, green means healing, and gold means support. Choose a different skill icon to see its targets. Only healing skills show green.
  3. Watch the recharge. After any order, that creature needs 2–3.5 seconds to act again. Specials also need their own 8–12 second recharge. Targets remain visible during recovery. A command entered within the final half-second queues once and runs when ready; its target shows an hourglass. Moving or losing either participant cancels an outdated order. Hover an icon or open creature details for its exact effect.
  4. Read the Ram. Foldhorn marks one square for three seconds. The striped lane shows its aim. The double ring marks impact. Dodge for no damage, brace to hold position, or interrupt by rooting or displacing the Ram.
  5. Protect a troop. Brace blocks up to 2 from the next hit for up to 3 seconds. Moving or attacking drops it. Specialist wards stay until spent. The stronger protection applies.
  6. Win your mode. Flag Capture keeps ownership after you leave. Zone Control counts troops within one orthogonal tile of each flag; tied zones are contested and do not score. On each scoring pulse, held flags give light. Reinforcement Clash also pays supplies. The HUD shows each side’s next gain. First Light opens with one central beacon and a 10-second opening pulse. Last Army Standing compares surviving health when time expires.
  7. Read the ground. Roots, thorns, rifts, cinders, sanctuaries, and prism barriers stay on the board for 10–12 seconds. Their drawn marks and countdown badges show exactly where they apply. Solid ◆ identifies your field and hollow ◇ the opponent’s. Hover, focus, or hold a target before releasing to preview a ground skill’s footprint.
  8. Build a formation. Connect three Brood creatures orthogonally for a living colony. Put Carnival attackers on opposite sides of an enemy for a pincer. Line up three Choir allies in a contiguous row or column for a ranged ward. Select a creature to see its formation links and tap its formation badge for details.
  9. Use your faction. Brood troops move farther from groves and recover there. Carnival scouts phase through enemy formations. Most Choir troops fly over obstacles and can land on water; the Choir’s wards protect nearby allies.
  10. Play together. Choose “2 players · same screen” for simultaneous local play. Player 2 has the top bar and Player 1 the bottom. In short landscape windows, Player 2 commands from the left and Player 1 from the right. Both can drag their own creatures with separate fingers. This mode has no AI.

Menu contains pause/resume, creature details, sound, the journal, and new battle. Only Reinforcement Clash permits extra purchases during battle. Other modes use your starting warband and creatures summoned by special abilities. Story and Relaxed give the AI a longer grace period and much slower decisions.

Player 1: 1 Move · 2 Attack · 3/4/5 Specials · G Brace.
Player 2: Q Move · W Attack · E/R/F Specials · T Brace.
Space pauses both armies · Esc cancels selection. Arrow keys move board focus; Tab and Enter also select tiles.
