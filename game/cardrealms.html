How to play & read cards
TradeCombatHealthDefenseDrawAllyScrap

Play cards to gain trade and combat. Buy new cards with trade, then spend combat to attack bases or your rival's health. Destroy their outposts before attacking their health.

Ally bonuses trigger with two cards of the same faction. Scrapping a card removes it permanently for its bonus. Bases stay in play and repeat their effects each turn.

Hover or focus any card for its artwork and rules. Tap i or press I to pin details. Escape closes them. Enter or Space plays, buys, or attacks with the focused card.
