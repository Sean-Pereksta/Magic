THE DESK SURVIVAL GUIDE

A tiny army. A clever plan.

01 / Turn trash into troops

Twigs cost 25 trash. Send them to trash cans: one twig gathers at a time, carrying 5 trash back to a desk each trip. Spread workers across cans.

02 / Build your little empire

Select a twig to place a desk (200), cabinet (75), printer (150), CD reader (175), or defensive mouse (175). Desks receive trash and train twigs. Cabinets add 8 supply. Printers train troops for 50 each.

03 / Outsmart the rival

Basic troops have 180 health and attack every 1.25 seconds. Use the longer fights to flank, reposition, and time abilities. Matching types can fight; each type refuses to target its exact counter. All combat troops can damage buildings and twigs. Destroy all rival desks to win.

Air lift and pointer defense

Build a CD reader with a twig (V), then summon CDs for 75 trash each (Q). Select troops and tap a friendly CD to board: six troops per CD, no twigs. Select one or more CDs, press Load / L for the nearest troops, or Drop / U then tap the destination. CDs fly there and unload together. Shift-click or box-select multiple CDs.

Build a defensive mouse (M) to fire computer pointers at enemy CDs, rocks, paper, and scissors. Hits deal damage immediately and keep damaging the target for a short time.

04 / Unlock something special

Research at a printer for 150 trash each. Each ability has its own key. Select upgraded troops, then use Q / Launch, W / Crumple, or E / Sweep. In a mixed squad, each key affects only its matching troops. Rock and scissors need a target click; paper activates immediately. Buttons show readiness and cooldown.

Q · Rock · Launch — leap toward the target in formation.
E · Scissors · Sweep — cut a short arc for 135 damage to paper; combine with your army to finish healthy targets.
W · Paper · Crumple — 3 seconds invincible and faster; cannot attack.

Build a little fortress

Select a Twig and choose Wall / Z. Each block costs 20 trash, takes 3 seconds, and has 1,200 health. Blocks snap together into solid barriers. Keep tapping adjacent grid spaces to extend a wall; your Twig finishes its current construction before working through its queued blocks. Choose Done placing or press Esc to leave placement mode. Select a block and choose Dismantle block to open a passage, with no refund.

Walls stop friendly and enemy ground troops. Aircraft and projectiles pass over the short barricades. Enemy troops can destroy individual blocks to make a breach. Reinforced mouse defenses now have 850 health, 360 range, and fire 28-damage pointers every 1.25 seconds, followed by 6 damage per second for 3 seconds.

Specialists and battle changers

A completed Printer unlocks the Prototype Workshop (275 trash, N). A finished workshop plus Printer unlocks the Shredder Laboratory (400 trash, O). Select either to train its three troops with Q / W / E. Each queue holds six orders; larger troops reserve more supply. Tier 2 ground specialists can board CDs; Tier 3 cannot.

F · Precision Snipe: click a visible Paper target, then protect the 1.5s aim.
I / J / K · Airplane: scout with 900-pixel vision, intercept aircraft, tap two or more patrol points, or distribute sacrificial crash impacts.
G · Force Field: one smaller 80-pixel dome per click, using the nearest ready mage. It pushes ground troops outside its footprint.
T · Triple Division: one divider per click; sort both armies into three contained family sectors.
Y · Crushing Roll: launch coordinated boulders. Paper halts them.
H · Shadow Path: drag a route and release, or tap waypoints and choose Finish route / Enter. Esc cancels targeting; Stop cancels an airplane crash before its final dive.

Distant field and division targets make the caster walk into range and sight, then cast. Snipers approach distant visible targets before aiming. Charges and cooldowns start at the cast; Stop / S or a new order cancels the approach. Abilities obey the family counters on every target. Domes block ground travel; division walls also block attacks. Flying units pass over ordinary domes.

Two new battlegrounds

Stapler Canyon: take the northern ruler bridge, flank along the winding southern crossing, or attack the neutral Stapler Gate to open the center permanently. Use ramps for the cliff lookouts.

Conveyor Chaos: travel with belts for a 45% speed boost; traveling against them is 25% slower. Hold troops beside a control station to capture it. Select your station to reverse its belt for 18 seconds. Aircraft ignore the belts.

05 / Scout and take the high ground

Unexplored ground is hidden by fog of war. Scouts reveal the map; explored ground stays dim when your troops leave, and enemies disappear out of sight. Ramps connect low ground to raised platforms. Cliffs block ground travel and low-ground vision; flying CDs can scout across them. Scout a site before building.

06 / You’re in command

Desktop: drag to select, Shift to add, right click to move, gather, or attack. A, then left or right click attacks toward an area. The Attack / A tile uses the same targeting. F2 selects all fielded combat troops; F3 selects idle workers. Move the mouse to a battlefield edge to pan, or use arrows and middle drag. Click or drag the minimap to travel quickly. Q/W/E train at a printer (R also trains scissors); Q trains a twig at a desk. Select troops or buildings, then hold 1–4 to assign a command group (or press Ctrl+1–4). Tap the key to select it; tap twice to center the camera.

Touch: hold still, then sweep to group select. Tap ground to move or enemies to attack. Choose Attack, then tap the destination to fight toward it. Drag to pan; pinch to zoom. To save a command group, select troops or buildings and hold a numbered box at the bottom. Tap the box to select the group; double tap to center the camera. × clears selection.
