Poker hands & how to play

Pick 1–5 cards. Only the cards forming your poker hand contribute their chip values. The blue Chips × red Mult is your score; jokers activate from left to right.

Beat the Small, Big and Boss blinds to advance an ante. Clear ante 8, then continue into endless play. Each blind reshuffles your remaining deck, including permanent card upgrades.

The shop sells playable cards that join your deck for the rest of the run. An edition badge marks a special effect. Jokers trigger first, then your Jesters, each from left to right.

Read each boss's rule before playing. Cash out to earn Medallions you can spend at Fortune Lodge between runs.

Earn the blind reward, $1 for each unused hand, and $1 interest per $5 saved (up to $5 before upgrades). You have two lives, four hands and three discards by default.

Enter: play · D: discard · 1–8: select cards · R: sort rank · S: sort suit · Escape: clear selection or close.
