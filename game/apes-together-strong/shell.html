Light means danger

Flashlights and tower beams reveal any ape they touch. Keep to shadows and trees. Humans shout, run to alarms, and call reinforcements by radio. Interrupt a caller before help arrives. Break sight and move away from the last place you were seen.

Free your kin

Captive apes wait in cages around human camps. Strike a cage until it breaks, then call with Q to gather the freed apes. A large horde can overwhelm a patrol, but a crowded road is easy to spot. Hold your tribe in safe cover and scout alone when the hunt is hot.

Give your tribe a home

Settle on safe open land near berry bushes. More inhabitants support a larger lodge, and more adults build faster and raise more young. Young apes grow up in 35 seconds. Families need food, safety, and spare housing; gardens help larger communities thrive. Open the map to choose balanced growth, food security, or fortification, and visit to deliver food. Assign patrols to defend your homes, and come back to rally more apes when you need them.

Turn the hunt against them

Healthy adult apes can take a few ordinary hits, but concentrated gunfire still tears through a tribe. Use cover, then strike together. Military facilities hold large groups of captives; liberating them also wins food supplies. Your king slowly recovers when safe. Later marksmen can kill an ape in one shot: watch their aiming line and move away from its endpoint. Orange circles warn of grenades. Flank shield guards or swarm them. Medics support nearby soldiers. Each passing night brings a harder hunt.

Playing by touch

Use the left stick to move and the right button to strike. Open Commands to rally, hold, recall, settle, or patrol. Choose Charge, then tap the ground to send the horde in that direction.
