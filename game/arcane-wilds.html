An isometric action RPG across a sprawling room-to-room world. Your hero auto-attacks, but survival depends on movement, dodging, spell timing, evolving a three-spell loadout, and building equipment from rare drops and hard-earned materials.

🕹 Dual analog controls✨ Branching spell evolution🗺 Expanding dangerous regions⚒ Material forging💍 3 trinket slots🏘 Living villages & quests
Three spells. Endless variants.Unlock only three active spells, then turn them into split shots, orbitals, chain effects, storms, detonations, summons, and more.
Rare gear has identity.Weapons, armor and trinkets can roll optional prefixes that alter power and suffixes that add special effects.
Villages live between battles.Moving villagers, merchants, blacksmiths, alchemists and quest keepers give the world places worth returning to.
Outer rooms fight back.Challenge rooms and advanced enemies use hooks, mortar zones, dashes, healing, lightning patterns, burrows and execution attacks with readable telegraphs.

Controls

Auto-attacks fire on their own. You control positioning, aim priority, spells and dodges.

MoveWASD / Arrow keys. On touch, use the left analog stick.
Aim priorityMouse position or right analog stick. Release it to let auto-attack pick the nearest target.
Cast spellsKeys 1–3 or tap the three spell buttons at the bottom.
DodgeSpace, right click, or DODGE. Dodging grants a brief invulnerable burst.
InteractE or USE near villagers, stores, shrines, caches and forges.
ExploreDefeat room enemies to unlock doors. Danger and rewards scale as you push farther from the starting village.

Level Up

Choose one spell. Owned spells open three evolution cards.

Evolve Spell

Choose one mutation. Upgrades alter how the spell behaves and can stack into strange builds.

Three-Spell Limit

Your new spell is unlocked in the spellbook. Choose which active spell it replaces.

Loot Found

Equipment is uncommon in the wild, but bosses and crafting offer better odds. Affixes are never guaranteed.

Spellbook & Gear

Review spells, weapon, armor, three trinkets, materials and active village quests.

Journey Paused

Your explored rooms and build are saved automatically.
