How the draft assistant thinks

The recommendation score starts with draft value, then adjusts for roster need, stack opportunities, positional over-investment and player availability risk.

GREEN

YELLOW

Questionable, limited, PUP or another situation worth monitoring.

RED

Out, IR, suspended or another major availability issue.

Fantasy points use full-PPR scoring: 1 point/reception, 0.1 per rushing/receiving yard, 0.04 per passing yard, 6 per rushing/receiving TD, 4 per passing TD, minus 2 per interception/fumble lost when those fields are available.
